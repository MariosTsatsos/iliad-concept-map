# Course material

Slides, notes, exercise sheets and solutions from the Iliad Intensive, by the Iliad team and the lecturers named on each day.
They are published here with Iliad's permission, so that the cards on the map can link to them.
The map lists them under each day; this page lists them all.

Files from the September cohort are listed under the November day with the same topic.
Books and papers by other authors are not copied here: the cards link to them where they are published.

Copyright stays with the Iliad team and the authors of each file.
