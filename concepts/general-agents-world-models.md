## In one sentence
Richens, Abel, Bellot and Everitt (2025): an agent that can achieve a wide enough range of multi-step goals must contain a predictive model of its environment, and that model can be extracted from its policy alone. The more capable the agent, the more accurate the extracted model.

## Twist
Agents trained without any explicit model are not exempt: competence itself implies a world model.

## Why it matters for safety
If capable agents contain world models, those models can be extracted and inspected.
