## In one sentence
In a mixed equilibrium the other player's probability is the break-even point for my payoff: at that value my two actions pay the same, so their choice sets my payoff, not mine.

## Twist
Marios derived for the game of chicken a payoff of the form 3q + m(1 - 2q): the term with m vanishes at q = 1/2.
