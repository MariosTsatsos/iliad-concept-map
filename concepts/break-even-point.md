## In one sentence
In a mixed equilibrium the other player's probability is the break-even point for a player's payoff: at that value the player's two actions pay the same, so the other player's choice sets that payoff, not the player's own.

## Twist
For the game of chicken the payoff has the form 3q + m(1 - 2q): the term with m vanishes at q = 1/2.
