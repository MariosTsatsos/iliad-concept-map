## In one sentence
In statistical physics, how an average responds to a small external field equals how it fluctuates together with the quantity the field couples to. The Bayesian influence function identity ∂E[φ]/∂β_i = −Cov(L_i, φ) is exactly this, with β_i as the field and L_i as its conjugate energy (Day 17 notes, Remark 3.3).

## Twist
Influence can be measured with no intervention at all: watch the fluctuations of the unperturbed posterior and read off the response.
