## In one sentence
Share the credit for a behaviour fairly: average an example's marginal effect over all the orders in which the examples could be added to the training set. It comes from cooperative game theory, where it splits a team's payoff among its players.

## Twist
Shapley values still dilute credit when examples are redundant (notes, Remark 1.5), and they cost far more than leave-one-out.
