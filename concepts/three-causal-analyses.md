## In one sentence
Day 17 slides: mechanistic interpretability is causal analysis of the forward pass (which circuits caused this output?); data attribution is causal analysis of the training run (which data caused this model?). Claude's extension: the three questions of Day 14 line up with them, approximation with the forward pass, generalisation with the data, optimisation with the path of training.

## Twist
Unrolling is backpropagation one level up: through the training run instead of through the network.
