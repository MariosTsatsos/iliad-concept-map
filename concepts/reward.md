## In one sentence
A reward is a number given for a single step. Four axioms on preferences give one utility number for a whole sequence of steps; a fifth axiom about time is what allows that utility to be split into a reward for each step plus a discounted utility of the steps that follow: u(t followed by h) = r(t) + γ(t) × u(h), where t is one step, h is everything after it, r is the reward and γ (gamma, between 0 and 1) is the discount.

## Twist
The fifth axiom says that putting the same step in front of two different futures shrinks the difference between them by a factor γ that depends only on that step. The Day 3 notes call it 'Temporal γ-indifference'; the slides call it 'Temporal equanimity'.
