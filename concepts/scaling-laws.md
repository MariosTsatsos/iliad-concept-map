## In one sentence
Test loss of language models falls as a smooth power law in parameters, data and compute, which let labs extrapolate from small runs.

## Twist
The laws were fitted without a mechanism, so they give no warning of where they break; and a smooth average loss hides abrupt jumps in single capabilities.
