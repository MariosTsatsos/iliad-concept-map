## In one sentence
An analogy from field theory: a symmetry of the loss gives directions in which nothing changes, zero modes, which appear as zero eigenvalues of the Hessian. Physicists handle them by fixing a gauge or adding a small mass term; the localisation strength γ of the Bayesian influence function, and the damping λ of the classical one, play that role.

## Twist
Seen this way, degeneracy is not a defect of neural networks but the expected sign of their symmetries.
