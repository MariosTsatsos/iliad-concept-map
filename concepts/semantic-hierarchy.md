## In one sentence
Trained on facts about animals and plants, a deep linear network first separates animals from plants, then birds from fish, then canaries from robins. Broad distinctions are the stronger modes of the data, so saddle-to-saddle dynamics learn them first (Saxe, McClelland and Ganguli, 2019).

## Twist
The order matches how children acquire concepts, and it comes from a model with no nonlinearity at all.
