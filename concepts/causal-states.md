## In one sentence
Two pasts belong to the same causal state when they give the same distribution over the whole future. In the slides' words, the causal states record every distinction that makes a difference. They are the minimal sufficient statistic for prediction, and the machine that moves between them as symbols arrive is called the ε-machine.

## Twist
A process of independent coin tosses has one causal state. The Golden Mean process, where two 0s in a row are forbidden, has two: was the last symbol a 0 or not.
