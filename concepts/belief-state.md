## In one sentence
For a process generated by an HMM, the belief state η gives the probability of each hidden state, given everything seen so far. Each new symbol x updates it: η' = η T(x) / (η T(x) 1). It is sufficient for predicting the entire future, and the next-token probabilities are a linear readout of it.

## Twist
Belief states need not be minimal: different beliefs can predict the same future. They are minimal when the HMM is minimal-dimensional among all GHMM realisations of its process.
