## In one sentence
Marios's five-step account of Day 8: (1) backdoors in software, (2) obfuscation of programs, (3) logic gates built inside neural networks, (4) backdoors in neural networks, (5) obfuscated weights.

## Twist
Each step uses the one before: because gates fit in a ReLU network, an obfuscated circuit with a backdoor is also a neural network with a backdoor.
