## In one sentence
A summary R of the past is sufficient for prediction if any two pasts with the same summary give the same probabilities for what comes next. It is minimal if it is the coarsest such summary: it merges every pair of pasts that predict the same.

## Twist
The raw past is always sufficient; the work is in throwing away everything that does not matter.

## Quiz
For a fair coin tossed independently, what is a minimal sufficient statistic of the past? (Answer: a constant. The past says nothing about the future.)
