## In one sentence
A perceptron whose weights are +1 or -1, learning from a teacher; as the ratio alpha of examples to weights grows, it jumps to perfect generalisation at alpha_c of about 1.45.

## Twist
Just below the jump the generalisation error is about 0.25; above it the error is zero. A phase transition in learning.
