## In one sentence
A perceptron whose weights are +1 or -1, learning from a teacher; as the ratio alpha of examples to weights grows, it jumps to perfect generalisation, at alpha_c of about 1.45 in the annealed approximation used in the notes.

## Twist
In that approximation the generalisation error is about 0.25 just below the jump and zero above it: a phase transition in learning. The notes state that the exact calculation puts the transition lower, at alpha of about 1.245.
