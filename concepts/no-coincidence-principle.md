## In one sentence
If something that would be an outrageous coincidence happens, there is a reason for it; the reason need not be a proof and can be a heuristic argument.

## Twist
For networks: if a network has a surprising property, such as never misbehaving, there is structure behind it, and that structure is what a watcher of training should find.
